# STORYBOARD — Nova Fox, the story arc

**THE GOAL — Will's canon (2026-11-26 15:29 CST, verbatim):** "Nova Fox is the story of the player rescuing an entire world from destruction, by collecting pieces of its heart that have been strewn across the multiverse." The Nova Heart, in pieces across the realms, is the quest spine; everything below serves it.

## Scene 1 — The Conquest

- **Visual:** Star charts unrolling over a settled galaxy; ships on
  charted lanes; every address known, every distance priced. Calm,
  finished, faintly boring — a civilization with nothing left to
  discover.
- **Narration intent:** Establish scale and completion in two or
  three sentences. Pride, but the tired kind. (Backstory — film and
  premise copy; the game opens later, at Scene 4.)
- **Player action:** None — this is the film/premise layer
  (see INTRO-VIDEO.md on what the current film covers).

## Scene 2 — The Dark

- **Visual:** The charts again — but most of the frame is unlit.
  Instruments sweep and report mass that isn't there: something
  outweighs everything they can render, and it stays dark.
- **Narration intent:** Name the one unsolved problem in the
  civilization's own word: **dark matter**. Not a villain — a fact
  they live with. The conquest's single asterisk.
- **Player action:** None yet. This scene is why anyone is sent
  anywhere.

## Scene 3 — The First Rift

- **Visual:** A place where the dark touches the visible: light
  behaving wrong, a thing arriving that the charts say shouldn't
  exist, an opening into somewhere addresses shouldn't reach. The
  first crack in the visible universe.
- **Narration intent:** Wonder before fear. *"And then they
  discover rifts"* — the anomaly the whole game turns on. What a
  rift *is* stays open (DESIGN G27); the storyboard plays it as
  discovery, not explanation.
- **Player action:** None — but this is the scene the game will let
  the player *do* in Scene 6, so the film should teach its shape:
  an opening you can see through, and step through.

## Scene 4 — Arrival (the game opens)

- **Visual:** A clearing in a first realm — **Nova Prime**. A fox
  wakes. A standing stone nearby carries a still image on its face
  (the intro film lives here, in-world, on the memory screen).
  Everything is quiet and learnable.
- **Narration intent:** The civilization sends people in; the
  player is one of them. The guide's first beat (recorded, N-01
  "wake") does this in the game's own voice — a person, not a
  manual. Narration is Emise's voice throughout (Will, Review 1
  item 11).
- **Player action:** Wake, look, take the first steps. (Beat:
  BEAT-arrival, N-01.)

## Scene 5 — The Instrument

- **Visual:** The guide shows the numbered instrument: play notes,
  three make a chord, a chord finds a rift. Numbers on the ground
  of the world become a way to travel it.
- **Narration intent:** One idea per beat, taught by doing:
  rifts exist (N-02), moving (N-03), the numbers (N-04). The guide
  speaks only when there's something worth saying, then hands over.
- **Player action:** Move, jump, play the day's tune, watch a
  pocket open. First shaping: the chisel — *"Aim at any block and
  press C — that's your chisel. Three strokes shape a block down
  and break it through — and that break stays, too."* (guide line,
  in game today).

## Scene 6 — The Crossing

- **Visual:** A rift rendered as a true window: through the ring,
  another realm in its own colors (Ashfall's embers), its ground,
  its standing things, its weather of light — live, not a picture.
  The player steps through and the world changes palette whole.
- **Narration intent:** The crossing is the game's first true
  thing and the guide treats it that way (N-05 "bridge", N-06
  "ashfall"): you are somewhere the charts are new again. Other
  realms exist — eight more names on the chart — and every one of
  them looks like itself.
- **Player action:** Open the rift, look through it, cross. Meet
  the first thing of the dark; first fight; the Lance found where
  the fiction puts it.

## Scene 7 — Down Is Not a Law

- **Visual:** The player flips down upside down; a dark ceiling
  that wasn't there a moment ago generates overhead to catch them;
  the world walks on its sky.
- **Narration intent:** The guide's gravity beat (N-07, in game
  today): *"Down isn't a law here — each place keeps its own
  down, and it remembers what you set."* Story reason, for the
  storyboard's later scenes: near the rifts, the dark presses in —
  and where it presses, down wavers. This is the fiction's seed
  for the gravity dial (GRAVITY-DIAL.md).
- **Player action:** Flip (G), fall up, land on the ceiling, flip
  back. Learn that every realm remembers its own down.

## Scene 8 — The Deep Room

- **Visual:** A hill, a shaft in its top, a barrow hall below; a
  keeper that has guarded one stone a long time; the Deep Stone
  itself — the thing that will not shape, will not move.
- **Narration intent:** The quest beat (N-08) in the guide's
  plainest voice: *"Climb the hill and go down the shaft in its
  top. Fight with click or F. And watch your hearts — when they're
  gone, you're carried back to where you started."* The guardian
  keeps its own spoken line; everything else is effort and noise.
- **Player action:** Descend, fight the Keeper, reach the Deep
  Stone. The reward is written down and stays: **a hat is
  granted** — the game's first prize, and the template for every
  prize after it.

## Scene 9 — The Wider Dark (the promise the launch sells)

- **Visual:** The chart again — but now the player has stood in
  two of its realms. Eight more names wait, each a color they
  haven't seen from inside. A new pocket opens somewhere every
  day; yesterday's world is saved whole and can be revisited.
- **Narration intent:** The arc doesn't close; it widens. The
  conquest is finished, the dark is not — and the player is now
  one of the people the civilization sends into it, every day.
  This scene is the store page's job as much as the game's: it's
  what the wider world (PLAN §3) looks like from inside the story.
- **Player action:** Choose a direction: a new realm, the day's
  pocket, a mark to leave where others will find it.

## How this maps to what exists

- Scenes 4–8 are **in the game today** (guide sequence, crossing,
  gravity beat, quest — V5-REPORT item 19; narration recorded,
  27 beats, Aria pipeline).
- Scenes 1–3 are **backstory**: they live in the premise (DESIGN
  G26 — how much surfaces in-game is Will's open ruling, PLAN
  D10) and are the natural subject of a story cut of the intro
  film. The current film is an emblem film, not these scenes —
  see INTRO-VIDEO.md.
- Scene 9 is **the offer**: realms, daily cycle, and prizes as
  the fiction's own forward motion — storyboarded so launch copy
  and game story say the same thing.
