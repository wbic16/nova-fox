The ladder — power-ups for credits

Dozens of rungs toward a legend, bought once each and yours for good. This season's shelf is the white, grey, and green gear; the red and blue stand below, priced in the open, and come at Christmas with the dragon. Your form — a small phext you wear

Your shape is yours to write: a little phext, one page a frame, and the frames walk its coordinates in order — that walk is the animation. A page can be svg, a bitmap, a turtle's walk, or plain shapes; pages may mix. The ruled lines are 4 KB of phext and the size of your own body; the build is generous past both, and says so where you stand. What you wear is written to the shared map, so the form is your public shape. Form pictures are pictures — nothing in a form ever runs.

Speed runs

One run, in the world's own terms: arm at Arrival, the clock starts when you move, and the finish is standing at the Deep Stone in the barrow. Splits are taken at Ashfall, the barrow door, and the guardian's fall. A run never touches the shared world — your marks, your purse, and your standings all stand.

Stream your telemetry

Point the game at a WebSocket listener — an overlay, a friend's machine, your own — and your run flows to it live: starts, splits, positions, the finish. Nothing flows until you connect, and a dead listener never touches the run.
