The solver — earn credits by contributing

Lend this machine's spare strength to work the world needs — checking the living lattice, in small verified pieces — and the world pays you in credits. The solver never takes more than 30% of your machine; the meter below is the same measurement that holds it there. An honest hour of contributing earns about 2.45 credits — so a 100-credit gift for a friend is about 41 hours of your machine's spare time. It runs only while you ask it to. The stop button stops it now; machines sleep, laptops close, and nothing is owed for work left unfinished.
the solver is resting.

The ladder — power-ups for credits

Dozens of rungs toward a legend, bought once each and yours for good. This season's shelf is the white, grey, and green gear; the red and blue stand below, priced in the open, and come at Christmas with the dragon. Nothing here is the only way to do anything — every rung widens a verb you already have.

The field guide — what things are for

Every thing you can hold, what it does, and how you use it — the weapons and charms out in the world, and the ladder's rungs below.

The gravity dial — Morse, and how hard down pulls

The dial is a small subspace. Key Morse on it to choose your next coordinate. S.O.S (... --- ...) brings you home. Joy teaches the marks as you walk — S and O first, then the rest of the alphabet and the digits. The flip stays free, once a realm each day. The full sweep of magnitude and frequency belongs to a bought key, or to whoever kills the dragon.

What you dig — material and placing

Digging has a point now: what the chisel breaks, you carry. Place a carried block and it stands in the world for every fox, for good — the same shared record as a carve, spent from your pack. What you cannot use, the world buys back at the rates below.

Your form — a small phext you wear

Your shape is yours to write: a little phext, one page a frame, and the frames walk its coordinates in order — that walk is the animation. A page can be svg, a bitmap, a turtle's walk, or plain shapes; pages may mix. The ruled lines are 4 KB of phext and the size of your own body; the build is generous past both, and says so where you stand. What you wear is written to the shared map, so the form is your public shape. Form pictures are pictures — nothing in a form ever runs.

Speed runs
